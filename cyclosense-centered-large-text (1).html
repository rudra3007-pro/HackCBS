Cyclones move fast. Warnings need to move faster.

Tropical cyclones threaten coastal lives and livelihoods every season. Better monitoring means more time to prepare.

Vast ocean, limited visibility

Early-stage systems form over open water and are hard to spot across huge volumes of satellite data.

Manual analysis takes time

Interpreting imagery by hand slows down classification when every hour matters.

Preparedness gaps

Late or unclear information makes it harder for authorities and communities to act in time.

Learning from the sky, to protect the ground.

Cyclosense applies AI/ML to satellite and ocean-atmosphere data to support three tasks that decision makers rely on.

Identify

Detect tropical cyclone patterns in incoming data.

Classify

Assign intensity categories to detected systems.

Predict

Estimate likely development to support early planning.
