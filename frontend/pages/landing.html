The problem we are solving

Tropical cyclones over the North Indian Ocean cause billions in damage and claim thousands of lives each year. Current manual monitoring processes are slow, resource-intensive, and prone to delays in early warning dissemination.

Late detection

Manual satellite image review introduces lag between cyclone formation and alert issuance.

Inconsistent classification

Intensity categorisation varies between analysts, leading to uncertain preparedness levels.

Poor track forecasting

Short forecast horizons leave coastal communities insufficient time to evacuate safely.

Our three-stage AI approach

Each stage is an independent, swappable deep learning module — detect, classify, then forecast.

Stage 1

Detection (YOLOv8) — Scans raw satellite imagery to locate and confirm the presence of a tropical cyclone system at pixel level.

Stage 2

Classification (EfficientNet-B4) — Categorises detected systems into IMD intensity classes: D, DD, CS, SCS, VSCS.

Stage 3

Forecasting (Bi-LSTM) — Predicts the next 5 track positions (15-hour horizon) from the past 8 historical fix points.

Platform features

A unified dashboard that brings together detection, classification, forecasting, mapping, and AI-powered advisory in one interface.

Satellite image upload

Upload any JPEG/PNG satellite image and get instant YOLO-based cyclone detection results.

Intensity classification

EfficientNet assigns an IMD category with probability bars for all five intensity classes.

Live Leaflet map

Interactive North Indian Ocean map with animated track, forecast cone, and landfall markers.

Track & intensity forecast

LSTM model predicts the next five positions and wind-speed timeline from 8-point history.

City proximity alert

Search any coastal city to see its distance from the projected track with a hazard rating.

Indoor Safety AR Scanner NEW

Scan your room using device camera to detect hazardous glass windows and pinpoint reinforced shelter zones with voice guidance.

Nearest Safe Shelters Radar NEW

Acquires GPS coordinates to locate verified MPCS coastal cyclone shelters, elevation, storm surge safety, and optimal evacuation route.

AI Cyclone Copilot

Real-time meteorological & civil defense advisory powered by Groq LLM with emergency response protocols.
